Phishing attacks are getting more sophisticated, but there are almost always subtle tells. In this post, we'll go over the top 5 signs to look out for.

## 1. Urgency and Fear
Attackers want you to act fast without thinking. If an email claims your account will be deleted in 24 hours, be highly suspicious.

## 2. Generic Greetings
Instead of your actual name, the email might say "Dear Customer" or "Valued Member." This is a classic sign of a mass phishing campaign.

## 3. Suspicious Links
Hover over any links before clicking. Often, the visible text will say "paypal.com" but the actual URL points to something like "paypaI-security.com".

> Always check the domain carefully!
